Model and sampling

The surface shows the magnitude of the summed horizontal force on the whole jacket, with independent wave and current headings. It uses the existing Part 2(a) linear wave–current model and integrates drag and inertia to the instantaneous local wave surface.

Wave height 12 m · period 10 s · water depth 40 m · current speed 1.6 m/s. Headings are sampled every 12°, with 100 phases per cycle and 129 integration points along each submerged member. This display grid does not replace the refined maximum search.

Column drag uses the horizontal velocity vector. Beams and diagonals retain the independent normal-component drag approximation in the calculation code. Positive headings rotate from +x towards +y.
